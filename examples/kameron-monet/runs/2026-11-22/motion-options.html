Motion options for the news panel

Three takes on the top half of the split screen for each story. Every one moves once, freezes, then keeps a small idle loop so the eye stays on it while the clip plays underneath. Each take also tries a different colour world, one dark, one light and one saturated, so colour can be judged alongside the idea.

The clips start muted and restart with the graphic. Use the Sound button above a frame, or click the frame, to hear it. One plays sound at a time. Captions run along the bottom of each clip. For the last two seconds a small Watch the full video pop-up appears in the top right of the clip, in four looks rotated across the frames.

  • Simple one quiet move
  • Exaggerated the same idea, turned up
  • Funny a visual joke
The lower half is a stand-in for the clip, which plays untouched with nothing laid over it. Everything in the news panel is typeset or drawn from plain shapes, with no screenshots or photos of the people in the story. The palette name sits beside each label, so a colour and an idea can be picked separately.
